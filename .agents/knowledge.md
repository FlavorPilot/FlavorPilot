# Culinary model and dataset

The checked-in source dataset is a transcription of the v0.3.0 project, not measurements or an
expert endorsement. The transcription fixture is a regression guard, not an independent source.
No published culinary evidence validates its numbers. Do not expand it by generating random
0–10 values. Capture provenance, review status and rights for future data additions.

Kernel values are deterministic heuristic outputs, not predicted taste success or food safety.
CUL-001 is a real inherited warning limitation. A source behavior can be regression-tested while
still being a poor culinary model. Keep that distinction in tests, documentation and UI.
